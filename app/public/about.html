About fireIQ

A YouTube research tool built to show off Firecrawl Alexandria

fireIQ is a demo. It shows what you can build with Firecrawl Alexandria: keyword trends, top videos and title ideas, all running on your own Firecrawl account. It's still a work in progress, so features and results may change.

What it shows off

Each part of fireIQ is a Firecrawl call made from the browser.

Rising and breakout keywords

What people are starting to search for on YouTube around your topic, and what's already searched most.

Alexandria · Firecrawl Trends

Interest over time

Daily search interest for a keyword, or up to five compared on one scale.

Alexandria · Firecrawl Trends

Outlier and hot videos

YouTube's top videos for a keyword, ranked by views or by views per day, with outlier badges.

Firecrawl scrape

Title suggestions

A language model reads the most-viewed videos for a keyword and writes new titles in the patterns that earn views.
